About the workshop

Ask Dom. He'll tell you what it needs.

Bundoora Radiator Service is an owner-run cooling workshop on Beatrice Avenue in Heidelberg West. Dom and the crew look after radiators, heater cores and the hoses and pumps around them.

Customers come back for the same reasons: the work is explained in plain terms, options are laid out before anything is replaced, and the car is checked over properly while it's in. Older vehicles, classics and engine conversions are welcome.

What we work on

Cooling, start to finish

Bring the car in, or call first and describe the symptoms. If it's not safe to drive, ask about getting it towed straight to the workshop.

RAD / REPAIR

Radiator repairs

Leaks, splits and blocked cores diagnosed, with the options explained before work begins.

RAD / REPLACE

Radiator replacement

Quality aftermarket radiators sourced to suit the vehicle, supplied fitted or for you to fit with guidance.

HTR / CORE

Heater cores

Heater core replacement when the cabin heat fails or coolant goes missing inside the car.

CUSTOM / MOD

Custom & modified

Original radiators adapted for engine swaps and conversions, including changes to inlets, outlets and header tanks.

HOSE / TRANS

Hoses & coolers

Leaking radiator and transmission cooler hoses replaced, plus pumps and related parts found during inspection.

FLEET / CLASSIC

Fleets & older cars

Ongoing cooling-system work for business fleets, and care for early-model and classic vehicles.
